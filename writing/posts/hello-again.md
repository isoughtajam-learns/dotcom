Being primarily a backend engineer who has worked mostly on full stack internal tools
these last 16 years has not made it easy to point to my efforts publicly. Coming of age
in the South Bay during the dot com era and starting my career in the Web 2.0 era,
I've had my fair share of FOMO looking at people create beloved pieces of software.

In middle school and high school I built several LAMP stack personal blogs that never
made it past my Bondi blue iMac. My first job in the internet economy involved SQL
programming and automated Sharepoint report generation based on a Netezza data
warehouse. Around the time Facebook gave up on PHP, I started learning Python and
Django on the job and thus began the golden years. 

## Looking forward

I recently got a taste for building public-facing features for a large, active user base
and it feels great. Maintaining software projects under public scrutiny is the best way
to keep one's skills sharp, so there will be more of that to come that I will proudly
share here.

Ironically, one of the skills that now requires regular sharpening is AI-native
development. And [despite](https://www.wheresyoured.at/make-fun-of-them/) 
[a laundry](https://fieldreport.caes.uga.edu/publications/TP121/how-data-centers-impact-surface-and-ground-waters/) 
[list of](https://kaporfoundation.org/datacenters-envt-health/) 
[concerns](https://www.brennancenter.org/our-work/research-reports/dangers-unregulated-ai-policing)
my professional life seems to depend on the mastery of AI usage. The effect on productivity
is undeniable with certain types of tasks, especially in software development. To that end,
this site and the projects I share will attempt to thoughtfully make use of various AI 
technologies.

## Housekeeping

This site itself it pretty simple-- HTML, CSS, Javascript. It makes use of [Eleventy](https://www.11ty.dev/) 
to render Markdown into these blog posts. It also provides an [RSS feed](/feed.xml), which is one
of the greatest technologies of the internet age.

It's hosted on [CloudFlare Pages](https://pages.cloudflare.com/) to make life easy,
and I'm developing using [WebStorm](https://www.jetbrains.com/webstorm/) as I am a
long standing member of the Jetbrains cult. So far Claude and Antigravity have been my
assistants on this and many other projects.
